never been done

A skate coach that fits in your pocket: a map of every trick, a session counter, your clips and a plan for what to learn next.

The trick map

215 tricks on one city-style map — 10 segments, from Basics and Ollies to Flip Tricks, Grinds, Slides, Manuals, Old School, Transition and Airs. Each line runs from the easy version to the hard one, so you always see what comes next.

Landed, then on lock

Mark a trick landed the first time it goes down. Land it on three different days and it goes on lock — proof that you have it any day, not just once. Fakie, nollie and switch count separately.

Sessions, clips, quests

  • Count tries and makes with one thumb — NBD keeps your makes in a row.
  • Film your makes or import them, right on the trick. Keep tutorials and example videos next to it.
  • Weekly quests point at the next real step. Rain, injury or the off season is a pause, not a lost streak.
  • XP, levels, eight ranks from Grom to SOTY and 50 stickers to collect.

Yours, on your phone

No account, no ads, no feed. Your map, clips and sessions stay on your phone. Privacy policy.
